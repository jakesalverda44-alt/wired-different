How pricing works

No hourly billing. No surprise invoices.

You should not have to book a call to understand how you will be charged. Here is the whole arrangement, before you talk to anyone.

Step 1 — the call

Thirty minutes, free. We map how your business actually runs and what is costing you the most. You leave with a build map whether or not you hire me.

Step 2 — the proposal

A fixed scope with one price and one timeline, in writing. You approve it before any money moves. Nothing starts on a handshake and a guess.

Step 3 — the build

A deposit starts it, the balance is tied to agreed milestones. Scope changes get their own price and your approval first.

What you keep

Code, hosting, domain, and data in your name. No per-seat licensing, no monthly fee to keep using what you paid for.

Typical starting points. Your proposal is scoped to what you actually need — these are floors, not packages.

Good to know
What does a build cost?

Every build is scoped after the call, and you get a fixed-scope proposal — one price, one timeline — before any deposit. No hourly billing and no surprise invoices. If the scope changes later, you approve the change and its price before I build it. If what you need does not justify the spend, I will tell you on the call rather than sell you a project.

How long does it take?

The timeline comes with the proposal. Sites move fastest. A custom CRM is scoped in phases, so you're using working software early instead of waiting for a big reveal.

Who does the work?

Jake does — all of it. There is no account manager and nothing gets handed to a subcontractor. You scope it with him, you build it with him, and you can reach him directly while it is being built.

Do I own it?

Yes, and this is set up that way from day one rather than transferred later. The code, the hosting account, the domain, and the data are all in your name. If you ever want to take the whole thing to another developer, nothing is holding it hostage. There is no per-seat licence and no monthly fee to keep using what you paid for.
